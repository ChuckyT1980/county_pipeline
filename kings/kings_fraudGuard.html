    We have moved to an updated version of Fraud Notify. In order to view/edit your existing alerts, please "Sign Up" with your previous email to retrieve your alerts.

    Fraud Notify is a simple way to help you prevent from being the victim of property fraud. Register your name and when a document is recorded with your name on it you will be notified via email. This gives you early warning of property fraud being committed in your name.
