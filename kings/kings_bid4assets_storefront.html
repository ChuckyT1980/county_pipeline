The Treasurer-Tax Collector's Office of Kings County, California is offering 64 tax defaulted properties for auction online.

Auction Dates

Starts: March 10, 2017 at 11:00 AM ET (8:00 AM PT)

Ends: March 14, 2017*

*Closing times vary; check listings for details.

Bids start as low as $600
These are all NO RESERVE auctions! Preview Now!

Contact

For questions about the auction and the bidding process, please contact service@bid4assets.com

Additional county information can be obtained at the Kings County Website

For questions about the assets and information that is not listed in the auction, please contact the county at tax.sale@co.kings.ca.us

To search for property by APN number, please use our search feature located on the right hand side of this page.

Deposit Requirements

A single $5,000 deposit (plus a $35 processing fee) is required to participate in the Kings County Tax Sale. Your deposit must be received by Bid4Assets NO LATER THAN 4:00 PM ET (1:00 PM PT) Monday, March 6th. Please make your deposit well in advance of the auction in order to ensure your eligibility to bid.

Disclaimers

Research and Inspect Thoroughly Before You Bid
Prospective purchasers are urged to examine the title, location and desirability of the properties available to their own satisfaction prior to the sale. The County of Kings assumes no liability for any other possible liens, encumbrances or easements, recorded or not recorded.

Properties are sold "AS IS"
The sale of these properties should not, in any way, be equated to real estate sales by licensed salespeople, brokers or realtors. The County of Kings makes no guarantee, expressed or implied, relative to the title, location or condition of the properties for sale. Property taxes currently due will be paid from the proceeds of the sale.

Important Disclaimer
The County of Kings is not liable for the failure of any device that prevents a person from participating in any sale. “Device” includes, but is not limited to, computer hardware, a computer network, a computer software application, and a computer Web site.

Please Note
Prospective purchasers are advised that some bonds or other assessments which are levied by agencies or offices other than the treasurer-tax collector may still be outstanding after the tax sale; in addition, the I.R.S. has the option of redeeming, up until 120 days after the sale, any property on which there is an I.R.S. lien recorded.

You must be at least 18 years old to bid!

ALL SALES ARE FINAL
