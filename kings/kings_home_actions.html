Owner Alert

Welcome to Owner Alert provided by Kings County and powered by Tyler Technologies.

Owner Alert is a simple way to help prevent you from being a victim of property fraud.  Register with your information, login after registered to add the ownership name(s) for your property(ies), and when a related document is recorded you will be notified via email.  This gives Kings County citizens an early warning of potential real estate fraud.

There is no charge for this service.
