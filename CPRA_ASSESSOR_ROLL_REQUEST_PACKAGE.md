## How to Send — Step by Step

**For each county:**

1. **Email** the template above to the assessor email in the table
2. **BCC** yourself at mrt@logicflowsystems.io so you have a timestamp
3. **Log the sent date** in the "CPRA Sent" column above
4. **Calculate response due date** — 10 calendar days from send date
5. **Follow up on day 11** if no response — they are legally required to respond

**If they don't respond within 10 days:**
> *"This is a follow-up to my public records request dated [DATE]. Per Government Code §6253, your office was required to respond within 10 calendar days. Please advise on the status of my request for the assessment roll in electronic format pursuant to R&TC §408.3."*

**If they claim a fee:**
> *"Per Revenue and Taxation Code §408, the right of public inspection of the assessment roll is free of charge. Per §408.3, electronic copies shall be provided at a cost not exceeding the direct cost of duplication. Please advise the specific cost and statutory basis before proceeding."*

## What You Get Back

Each county will send you a file — typically Excel or CSV — containing:
- Every parcel in the county (secured roll)
- APN, owner, situs address, land value, improvement value, total value

**When you receive a file:**
1. Drop it in `county_pipeline/<county>/assessor_roll_<county>_<year>.csv`
2. Run the join script: `python tax_pipeline/join_assessor_roll.py --county kern`
3. The script matches APNs to your auction parcels and replaces estimated values with real values
4. Re-export the Excel workbook with verified data
