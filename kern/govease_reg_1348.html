County Instructions

TAX DEED AUCTION - STANDARD

THERE WILL BE A 2 MINUTE CLOCK RESET ON EACH PARCEL

 

A $5,000.00 deposit is required for this auction. This deposit must be paid before getting approval.

The only payment method for this auction is Domestic Wire. Registration will be open from February 1st at 8:00am Pacific to March 4th at 5:00pm Pacific. Deposits can be submitted via Domestic Wire only until Wednesday, March 4th. NO DEPOSITS WILL BE ACCEPTED AFTER MARCH 4TH.

California Transfer Tax will be added to the total winning bid amount, and this amount will be shown on the live bidding page. Please do not submit your final payment until all fees have been applied and an email has been recieved. *Final payments can only be paid via Domestic Wire and must be received no later than Wednesday, March 18th by 5:00pm PST/7:00pm CST.* Please email support@govease.com for Wire Instructions.

***Vesting Information*** 

Bidders will be required to submit an initial Vesting Information form when registering. Winning bidders will have the opportunity to update their Vesting Information at the conclusion of the auction. If the Vesting Information is not updated, the original Vesting Information will be utilized by Kern County. 

 

***REFUNDS***

Refunds will take place 7-10 business days after the sale is completely finished. In the event a refund is required, these refunds will automatically be issued to the information filled out on the GovEase Wire Return Bidder Confirmation form.

 

***BIDDING PROCESS*** 

Once Live Bidding opens, your pre-bids will be placed and they cannot be removed! You can raise your bid on parcels during Live Bidding, and you can bid on new parcels at any time during Live Bidding. But you cannot delete your bid on a parcel during Live Bidding if you are currently winning.

All parcels will open at 8:00am Pacific on Monday, March 9th and will begin closing in 15 minute batches beginning at 8:00am Pacific on Wednesday, March 11th. Each batch will contain 25 parcels, except for the last batch which will contain 10 parcels. This number is subject to change as the result of any list removals.

If a property is currently taking live bids, there will be a two minute clock reset and the property will not close until all live bidding has ceased. Winners will be finalized by 5:00pm Pacific on Wednesday, March 11th. 

 

No Bidding Accounts designated for this auction! Please designate at least one bidding account to participate in this auction in order to register.
